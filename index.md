What’s inside

01

### Location hierarchy

Codes and display names for every Norwegian geographic unit (ward,
municipality, county, region and nation), with functions to map between
levels.

02

### Population by age

Annual population counts (1 January) by location and age category, from
national totals down to individual municipalities.

03

### Redistricting weights

Proportional weights that convert data recorded under old municipal
borders to the 2024 boundaries. The weights cover mergers and splits
from 2006 onward.
